Phase 2: Timeline / Implementation

Project duration: October 1 – November 20

WEEK 1 | OCT 1–7

Reviewing the survey results

  • Go through the 31 survey responses.
  • Identify the most common problems.
  • Decide which topics need more attention.
WEEK 2 | OCT 8–14

Planning the Digital Media Expo

  • Discuss activities and learning stations.
  • Divide tasks among group members.
  • List materials and resources needed.
WEEK 3 | OCT 15–21

Creating the expo materials

  • Make posters, presentations and other materials.
  • Prepare short videos, quizzes and QR codes.
  • Gather info about AI, online safety, scams and misinformation.
WEEK 4 | OCT 22–28

Testing the activities

  • Try out activities with group members.
  • Check presentations, quizzes and QR codes work.
  • Make changes where needed.
WEEK 5 | OCT 29 – NOV 4

Preparing for the expo

  • Complete and organize all learning materials.
  • Check equipment and resources.
  • Make sure everyone knows their responsibilities.
WEEK 6 | NOV 5–11

Coordinating and reviewing

  • Review the activities and presentations.
  • Coordinate with participating students.
  • Check for missing materials or tasks.
WEEK 7 | NOV 12–19

Final checking and preparation

  • Double-check all materials and activities.
  • Make final adjustments to presentations and stations.
  • Make sure everything is ready.
NOVEMBER 20 | FINAL IMPLEMENTATION

Project culmination

  • Conduct the Digital Media Expo with all learning stations.
  • Discuss AI content, cybersecurity, scams, misinformation and responsible tech use.
  • Quizzes, discussions and interactive activities.
  • Gather feedback and evaluate against survey problems.
  • Final group discussion: what went well, what we learned, what to improve.
