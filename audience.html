Target Audience

Target group: Senior High School (SHS) students

Why this population

  • Diverse prior knowledge and specific strand specializations.
  • Easier to interview and evaluate compared to lower grade levels.
  • Helps identify which groups require the most focus and intervention.

Priority digital needs

  • Difficulty accessing school platforms/portals (LMS) due to interface unfamiliarity.
  • Exposure to online scams, phishing, and predators beyond cyberbullying.
  • Lack of broad digital literacy and parental/guardian guidance.
  • Infrastructure barriers: limited personal devices, rural "last-mile" schools, and electricity instability.
  • Health impacts: screen time and device use before bed affecting sleep and class focus.
