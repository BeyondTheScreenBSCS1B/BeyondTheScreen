Phase 5: SDG Connection

This project supports 3 of the UN Sustainable Development Goals. It encourages digital literacy, responsible use of technology and creates a safe online environment. Being digitally responsible means knowing how to use technology and being aware of its effects on our education, health and general life.

How is this related to the SDGs?

4

SDG 4: Quality Education

Better digital knowledge and skills. Students learn to identify AI-generated content, recognize misinformation and use technology responsibly. Supports Target 4.4 (youth/adult skills) and 4.7 (digital citizenship and critical thinking).

Problem: lack of digital literacy, misleading information, limited understanding of responsible AI use.

Solution: fact-checking and ethical AI stations.

3

SDG 3: Good Health and Well-being

A healthier relationship with technology. Students become mindful of screen time, understand the effects of cyberbullying and practice healthier digital habits.

Problem: excessive screen time, sleep disturbances, reduced concentration, cyberbullying.

Solution: digital wellness tracking for sleep quality and mental focus.

16

SDG 16: Peace, Justice and Strong Institutions

A safer, more responsible digital community. Students recognize scams, suspicious activity and fake news, and verify before sharing.

Problem: scams, cybercrime, cyberbullying, misinformation, low awareness of online safety.

Solution: QR code phishing challenges for instant link verification.

Problems addressed

  • Cyber threats & scams (SDG 16): phishing, cybercrime and online predators threaten personal safety.
  • Disinformation (SDG 4 & 16): weak evaluation skills leave students open to fake news and manipulation.
  • AI & plagiarism (SDG 4): over-reliance on AI undermines academic integrity and skill development.
  • Digital wellness (SDG 3): screen time and cyberbullying lead to poor sleep, screen fatigue and mental health issues.

With Beyond The Screen: Think Before You Click we highlight how technology can be used responsibly to create a better community. We ultimately want students to become responsible digital citizens who think critically, make informed decisions and use technology in a way that benefits themselves and those around them.
