The Campaign

Beyond The Screen: Think Before You Click is a campaign aimed to raise awareness around digital literacy, tech responsibility and critical thinking. Due to the continued development of artificial intelligence, it is becoming increasingly important to understand what is going on behind the screen.

This campaign will help you understand the difference between content created by humans and AI, raise your awareness to misinformation and deceptive digital content, and gain understanding of the benefits and potential risks of new technology.

Using education, awareness and being responsible with technology, we want to improve your skills and make you a smarter digital citizen. Technology should be used responsibly, we shouldn't take information for granted and we should be mindful when clicking.
