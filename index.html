Do you think you can protect yourself on the internet?

Our advocacy campaign that aims to promote digital awareness, responsible technology use, and critical thinking in today's rapidly evolving digital world.

Falling for fake news?

Not everything online is true. We teach you to check the source, the date, and whether other trusted sites report the same story.

Too good to be true?

Free prizes, urgent messages, and “limited time offers” are classic scam tricks. We show you the red flags before you click.

Not sure what to share?

One share can spread a lie fast. We promote pause, verify, then post, so you help stop misinformation instead of spreading it.
